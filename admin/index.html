🔄 Full reset

Wipes all messages, attachments and send logs of every user (exactly like the 6-hour reset). Accounts stay.

📭 Clear my own inbox

Clears only your own received and sent mail — keeps the database and disk lean without touching other users.

🧹 Delete orphan files

Attachment files left on disk that are not linked to any message (leftovers of past resets) are removed.

👻 Remove inactive accounts

Accounts with no activity for more than 10 days are fully deleted (same as the daily cron).

🧯 Smart purge by content type

Messages carrying an attachment of a given type are deleted entirely (message + file). “Texts” only deletes attachment-free emails.
